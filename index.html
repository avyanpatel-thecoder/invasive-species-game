Invasive Species Challenge

Can you tell which species belong in California and which ones can harm its ecosystems? Test your knowledge in this interactive nature game.

How to play

Choose plants or animals, then investigate six species in each round.

Scoring rules
+1 point for each invasive species you select.
−1 point for selecting a native species by mistake.
−1 point for missing an invasive species.
Earn +1 for each correct native-species quiz answer.

You'll complete three rounds and answer three quick quiz questions after each round.

Why it matters

Invasive species can spread quickly, compete with native species for resources, alter habitats, and disrupt food webs. Learning to identify them is one way to help protect California's biodiversity.
